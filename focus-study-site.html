What is Focus Study?

It is a study timer that does one job well. Pick how long you want to focus, press play, and get on with your work. The timer keeps running even when your screen is locked, and a sound tells you when the session is done.

There are no logins and no clutter. The app works fully offline and keeps everything on your phone.
